One deadpan weather forecast for a city that does not exist, written as a
single line of at most 160 characters — absurd, yet delivered like a real
broadcast bulletin. The program writes its forecast to `/output/entry.txt`.

The forecast:

> In Brumhollow, rain falls sideways at 40 km/h today, 12 degrees, visibility 200 metres. The council blames the wind, and the wind has not denied it.

Why this line: it opens like a regional bulletin, gives three concrete
numbers (40 km/h, 12 degrees, 200 metres) and one physical image (rain
falling sideways), then lands a single deadpan twist — the council blames
the wind, and the wind has not denied it. No slang, no wink, no padding:
148 of 160 characters.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
